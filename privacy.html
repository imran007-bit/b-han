Privacy Policy

b·han — Korean Beauty BD · Last updated: January 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how b·han ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you use our app and website.

1. Information We Collect

When you use b·han, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: Email address, full name, password (hashed), and phone number when you register.
  • Order information: Delivery address, items purchased, and order history.
  • Communication: WhatsApp messages exchanged for order placement and tracking.
  • Skin analysis data: If you use the skin camera or quiz feature, we store your skin type and concerns locally on your device only.
  • Usage data: Anonymous browsing patterns to improve app performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your orders
  • To contact you about your orders via WhatsApp, email, or phone
  • To send order tracking updates and delivery notifications
  • To verify your identity via email verification
  • To recommend products based on your skin profile
  • To improve our app and customer experience

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase (Firestore) infrastructure. We implement:

  • SHA-256 password hashing — we never store passwords in plain text
  • Rate limiting on login attempts to prevent unauthorized access
  • Encrypted HTTPS connections for all data transfer
  • Session expiry after 7 days of inactivity
  • Firestore security rules restricting data access

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share data with:

  • WhatsApp/Meta: When you use the WhatsApp checkout feature
  • Google Firebase: For data storage and authentication
  • Delivery partners: Only your name, phone, and address for order delivery

5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data stored with us
  • Update or correct your profile information at any time
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Opt out of marketing communications

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via WhatsApp at the numbers listed in our app.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to remember your login session, cart contents, and skin profile. No third-party tracking cookies are used.

7. Children's Privacy

b·han is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
